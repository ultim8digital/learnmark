CLASSJOT · REPORT CARD COMMENTS

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

ClassJot helps teachers jot short observations about their students and turn them into report-card comment drafts. It is published by Oleg Korunov (ultim8digital) for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

In short: no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising. ClassJot’s own code makes no network requests, and your notes stay on your devices unless you turn on iCloud sync to your own iCloud account.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected

The app collects no data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. That is why its App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.

What ClassJot stores, and where

Your classes, students (first names and last initials), observation notes, tags, report runs, comment drafts, parent-note drafts and settings are stored in the app’s own container on your device. We never receive them. On iPhone and iPad the store uses iOS Data Protection (class Complete: unreadable while the device is locked); on a Mac it stays in the app’s sandboxed container, protected by FileVault when that is on. An optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock covers the app.

Device backups

By default ClassJot’s data is excluded from iCloud Backup and from Time Machine. If you turn on “Include in device backups” in Settings › Data, your device’s own backups may include the app’s data. ClassJot also keeps up to two automatic backup files on the device (before a restore or before sync is turned on).

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, ClassJot uses Apple’s CloudKit to keep your data in your own private iCloud database so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can see it; student text is stored in encrypted fields. We have no access to that database. Turning sync off can also remove ClassJot’s data from iCloud.

Apple Intelligence and other Apple features

Comment drafts, tagging, splitting a note that mentions several students and parent-note drafts use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Student names are replaced with placeholders before a note reaches the model. ClassJot never uses Private Cloud Compute or any third-party AI service. Dictation is transcribed on the device and the audio is not saved. Translation of parent notes uses Apple’s on-device Translation. A photo of a class list is read on the device and not saved.

Apple’s system Writing Tools are available in ClassJot’s text editors. They follow your device’s Apple Intelligence settings, which may include Private Cloud Compute or an extension you enabled; you can turn them off for the whole app in Settings › Privacy › “Writing Tools in ClassJot”. The system may also download Apple’s speech, translation and Apple Intelligence language files.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition (only while you dictate a note), camera (only for a photo of a class list), and Face ID (only for the optional app lock). Each is requested when you first use the feature and can be turned off in Settings.

Purchases

ClassJot Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple under the App Store’s terms. We never receive your payment details or your identity; the app only checks with StoreKit whether Pro is active.

Sharing

ClassJot never sends anything by itself. Exports, copied comments and parent notes leave the app only when you share them through the system share sheet or copy them.

Children

ClassJot is a tool for teachers. It is not designed for children and collects no personal information from anyone. Teachers are asked to store first names and last initials only and to avoid full names in notes; check your school’s policy on student information on personal devices.

Deleting your data

Settings › Data › “Delete all data” removes every class, student, note, report run, comment and parent-note log and the automatic backups from the device (with iCloud sync on, from your other devices too). Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Classes can also be archived or purged at the end of the term.

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the contents of your message and use them only to reply. Please don’t include student information.

Changes

Any change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
