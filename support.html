How can we help?

Email ultim8digital@gmail.com with the app version (Settings › About), your device and system version, and what happened. Please don’t send student names or notes.

Try the app in two minutes

On the Class screen tap “Explore a sample class”. It loads 24 fictional students with notes and a report run. Open Reports › Term 1 report and tap Draft all.

Jot a note

Tap the round button, then type or dictate. ClassJot recognises the students you name and files the note under each of them; mention several students and the split review offers a note for each.

Apple Intelligence

Comment drafts, tagging and parent-note drafts need Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iPad mini with A17 Pro, iPad or Mac with M1 or later) in a supported language. Without it ClassJot works in comment bank mode: drafts from the comment bank that cite your notes, keyword tagging and template parent notes. Every draft is a starting point; review it before use.

ClassJot Pro

Free includes one class, unlimited notes, the comment bank, parent notes and AI drafts for 5 of your own students (unlimited on the sample class). Pro adds unlimited classes, report runs and AI drafts on all your devices with one purchase. Restore purchases in Settings › ClassJot Pro. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on iPhone and iPad, or in the App Store › your account › Subscriptions on a Mac.

Sync, backups and moving devices

iCloud sync is off by default (Settings › Sync). ClassJot keeps its data out of device backups unless you turn on “Include in device backups”, so back up at term end (Settings › Data › “Back up everything (JSON)”) and use “Restore from backup” on a new device.

Privacy

ClassJot has no accounts, servers or analytics, and its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. Read the privacy policy.

For your school’s IT team

Settings › Privacy › “For your IT team” lists how ClassJot handles student information and can be shared as text. See also the privacy policy.

If ClassJot can’t open its notes

Tap Try again. If it keeps failing, tap Contact support (the error code is copied for you). “Start fresh” sets the current data aside on the device; it never deletes it.
