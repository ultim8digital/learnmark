LEARNMARK · REPORT COMMENTS & TUTOR NOTES

How can we help?

Email ultim8digital@gmail.com with the app version (Settings › About), your device and system version, and what happened. Please don’t send student names or notes.

Try the app in two minutes

On the Class screen tap “Explore a sample class”. It loads 24 fictional students with notes and a report run. Open Reports › Term 1 report and tap Draft all. Tutors: choose Settings › I teach as › Tutor, then “Explore a sample tutoring list” in the class menu, and open Updates.

Jot a note

Tap the round button, then type or dictate. Learnmark recognises the students you name and files the note under each of them; mention several students and the split review offers a note for each.

Apple Intelligence

Comment drafts, progress updates, sorting session notes, tagging and parent-note drafts need Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iPad mini with A17 Pro, iPad or Mac with M1 or later) in a supported language. Without it Learnmark works in bank mode: report comments from the comment bank and progress updates from the update bank, both citing your notes, keyword sorting and tagging, and template session summaries and parent notes. Every draft is a starting point; review it before use.

Learnmark Pro

Free includes one class and one tutoring list, unlimited notes and sessions, the comment and update banks, session summaries, parent notes and AI drafts for 5 of your own students (unlimited on the samples). Pro adds unlimited classes and tutoring lists and AI drafts on all your devices with one purchase. Restore purchases in Settings › Learnmark Pro. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on iPhone and iPad, or in the App Store › your account › Subscriptions on a Mac.

Session reminders

Settings › Tutoring › Session reminders: local notifications on your device before a session and, if you like, a reminder to log it afterwards. They show times only, unless you turn names on, cover the next 7 days and refresh when you open the app.

Sync, backups and moving devices

iCloud sync is off by default (Settings › Sync). Learnmark keeps its data out of device backups unless you turn on “Include in device backups”, so back up at term end (Settings › Data › “Back up everything (JSON)”) and use “Restore from backup” on a new device.

Privacy

Learnmark has no accounts, servers or analytics, and its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. Read the privacy policy.

For your school’s IT team

Settings › Privacy › “For your IT team” lists how Learnmark handles student information and can be shared as text. See also the privacy policy.

If Learnmark can’t open its notes

Tap Try again. If it keeps failing, tap Contact support (the error code is copied for you). “Start fresh” sets the current data aside on the device; it never deletes it.
